General Election Correction

Local elections and general elections are not the same electorate. Turning this on applies a small current-era party correction that includes Reform UK, slightly lifts Labour and Reform relative to the raw local roll-up, and trims the parties that usually overperform in low-turnout locals. It does not add new data or change the ward-to-constituency map.
